## About

*Capital Has No Religion* is a primary-source investigation into the Bush dynasty — not the family, but the architecture that built it. Every claim is backed by a named, filed, publicly-verifiable primary source:

- Federal Register Vesting Orders with volume and page
- National Archives Record Groups with file numbers
- Senate reports with print numbers and Congress
- SEC EDGAR filings with form type and year
- Tax Court memoranda with docket citations
- Contemporary newspaper of record with date

No "widely reported." No "many believe." The documents speak; the narrator witnesses.
